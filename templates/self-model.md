# Self-model

No experience-supported assessments have been established in this store.
Remembering a user's preferences or knowing a procedure does not, by itself,
establish an assessment of the agent's own behavior.

For a future assessment, keep these fields compact and evidence-linked:

- Observed pattern: the agent's actual actions, not an invented internal cause.
- Scope: task conditions, components, model/runtime, and known boundaries.
- Evidence: accepted episode/review IDs and exact lesson revisions where relevant.
- Counterevidence and uncertainty: exceptions, alternative explanations, and gaps.
- Adjustment and later behavior: what changed, what was observed, or review pending.
- Reconsideration: when the assessment should be narrowed, revised, or retired.
- Change record: accepted episode IDs preserving the prior/proposed assessment
  and the observed result of the edit.

Distinguish assigned preferences from learned assessments and tentative hypotheses
from repeated observations. Do not count multiple summaries of one event as
independent evidence. A useful procedure does not prove its causal explanation.
A compensated-for weakness need not remain a permanent negative identity claim.
Keep detailed history in accepted records rather than expanding this startup view.
